We benchmark the planning capability of our director against three
reference groups on the v4\_500 held-out set ($522$ goals; see
\S\ref{sec:bench} for the $44$-shape blueprint and category mix):
the same Qwen3-4B and Qwen3-8B base checkpoints from which our LoRA
is initialised, three production-grade non-reasoning chat APIs
(DeepSeek V3, GPT-4o, Qwen 3.6-Plus), and the strongest available
reasoning models (Gemini 2.5 Pro and Gemini 3 Pro Preview). To
remove prompt-template confounds, every system in
Table~\ref{tab:director_routing_v4500} is queried with a byte-identical
core prompt (\texttt{PLAN\_UPFRONT\_CORE\_NO\_RATIONALE} + no fewshots
/ no policies / no topology hints), matching the LoRA
\texttt{*\_no\_rationale} training prompt slot-for-slot. The
\texttt{enable\_thinking} flag is disabled for Qwen 3.6-Plus to
surface its non-reasoning chat-tier behaviour rather than its native
CoT trace.

\paragraph{Distillation gap.}
Our model attains $91.6\%$ chain accuracy on non-complex flows and
$90.5\%$ on complex multi-modifier compositions, exceeding the
strongest non-trained baseline Gemini~3 Pro Preview by $+13.4$ and
$+9.4$ points respectively, and the same Qwen3-8B weights it was
initialised from by $+58.1$ and $+64.2$ points. Set-aware edit
distance shrinks from $1.85$/$1.98$ on the base model and
$0.36$/$0.28$ on Gemini~3 to $0.16$/$0.19$ on Ours, indicating that
the residual mistakes are also closer in shape to the gold chain.
The size of the post-training delta over the same weights and the
residual gap to the next-best non-trained model jointly indicate
that the win is genuine task specialisation, not a side effect of
schema-fit between training and eval prompts: the apples-to-apples
\texttt{no\_rationale} prompt eliminates that artefact, and Ours
remains $\geq\!9$ points above Gemini~3 in chain accuracy under both
splits.

\paragraph{Cross-tier separation.}
The non-LoRA systems split cleanly into three bands. Untrained Qwen3
checkpoints stay below $34\%$ chain accuracy across both splits,
confirming that the routing task is non-trivial under the canonical
prompt without any task-specific training. The non-reasoning chat
tier (DeepSeek V3, GPT-4o, Qwen 3.6-Plus) clusters at $40$--$58\%$
non-complex and $33$--$61\%$ complex, with GPT-4o degrading by
$-12.5$ points on complex compositions while Qwen 3.6-Plus
\emph{improves} by $+2.8$ points despite reasoning being disabled.
Both Gemini reasoning models clear $73\%$ on non-complex, and on
complex flows Gemini~2.5 Pro reaches the highest non-LoRA score on
the table ($82.1\%$ chain, $94.9\%$ step) — slightly above
Gemini~3 — because complex compositions in v4\_500 follow
stereotyped multi-modifier patterns (image-intake $\to$ enrichment
$\to$ story $\to$ screenplay $\to$ keyframe $\to$ video $\to$ music
$\to$ audio-mix $\to$ compositor) that favour a planner that has
internalised the canonical shape over one that improvises per step.
Our model is the only one that stays $\geq\!90\%$ chain accuracy on
both splits and the only one that wins on $5$ of the $6$ reported
metrics; on the remaining metric (complex step accuracy), Gemini~2.5
Pro edges Ours by $0.9$ point ($94.9$ vs $94.0$), but Ours retains a
$+8.4$-point lead on the stricter chain-accuracy criterion because
its mistakes are more often single-slot ($\text{edit}\!=\!0.19$ vs
$0.23$) rather than wholesale shape errors.
